## ⑤ Cursive Font Generator → https://wpstackable.com/blog/how-to-engage-your-audience-with-dynamic-progress-graphs/

**评论草稿**：
> The animated progress bars are a nice touch. One typography note from working on font tools: when you overlay dynamic numbers on graphs, tabular figures (monospaced digits) prevent the jittering that proportional digits cause. Most Google Fonts have a tnum feature that people never enable.
>
> Small detail but it makes the difference between "looks alive" and "looks glitchy."

**为什么这么写**：肯定文章 + 补充一个字体领域的专业细节（tabular figures）+ 品牌植入自然（做字体工具的人给建议）+ 一句话总结有观点。
