## ③ AI Character Consistency → https://www.michaelagreiler.com/code-review-is-under-strain/

**文章摘要**：Dr. Michaela Greiler（代码审查领域研究员）写 AI 生成代码的速度超过了人类审查能力，传统 peer review 模式承压——AI 产生更大的 change set，reviewer workload 上升，没参与创建代码的人越来越难做 primary reviewer。

**评论草稿**：
> "Generating code is becoming faster than we can realistically read and review it" — this line captures the exact tension our team hit last quarter. We noticed reviewers started rubber-stamping large diffs they hadn't authored, which is worse than no review.
>
> One thing we've been experimenting with: AI-generated consistency checks BEFORE human review (same character/scene across generated images must pass automated similarity thresholds first). Shifts human attention to judgment calls only. Wondering if you see pre-review automation as part of the solution or if it just adds another layer?

**为什么这么写**：引用文章核心论点 + 分享自己团队的真实痛点（rubber-stamping）+ 把自己产品领域（AI 一致性检查）自然融入，且以提问收尾引导回复。
